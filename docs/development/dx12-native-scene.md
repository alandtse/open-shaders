# DX12 native scene handoff

`DX12NativeScene` loads an offline prepared interior package and publishes its matching completed acceleration structure. This does not establish dynamic-world coverage or validate that the package still matches the live scene.

The post-opaque raster capture exposes separate DX12 resources for raster-lit color, raster-lit specular, albedo, normal/roughness, masks, reflectance, motion, and raw depth. The lit resources retain Skyrim raster shadows. The motion snapshot precedes deferred composite writes and is not necessarily final motion. Depth is copied to R32_FLOAT without changing its depth convention; consumers must use the reverse-Z helpers.

A raster frame includes its source formats, physical dimensions, frame number, the producer fence and value, and a camera snapshot only when the current non-VR main-camera checks pass. The raster images are independent of prepared-scene readiness. Capture is unavailable if any required input layout or allocation is unsupported.

Borrow on the render thread with `GetRasterFrame(frame)`. A consumer must wait on the producer fence in its DX12 queue, transition resources from COMMON for use, return them to COMMON, and call `RetainRasterFrame` with its completion fence and value before releasing its shared frame. Keep the shared frame through submission. Unconsumed offers need no consumer fence. The two capture slots are reused only after producer and all registered consumers finish. No cross-frame fallback is published.

When enabled, the early mask uses the main perspective camera and prepared static point lights. It first attempts before raster shadow maps and retries at early prepass, world-render begin, and pre-opaque. Primary rays find prepared receivers; secondary rays query the prepared TLAS toward selected lights. Unsupported candidate geometry invalidates the sample. The mask is separate from raster-lit color and is never applied by this feature.

The RGBA32_FLOAT mask records weighted visibility in R, sample validity in G (1 traced, -1 eligible but not traced, 0 unavailable), primary ray distance in B, and prepared instance index plus one in A. Invalid pixels have neutral visibility 1. The current implementation traces full resolution and supports up to 32 prepared point lights. Prepared lights use offline baseline values; flags such as flicker, portal constraints, and reference overrides may be approximated rather than followed at runtime. Dynamic geometry, moving lights, complete material coverage, and full scene equivalence are not established. The combined visibility is weighted across lights, not a per-light signal. The feature currently supports the flat renderer only; VR falls back to the existing rendering path.

`GetSceneFrame(frame)` offers whichever current-frame outputs are available, with the prepared scene revision alongside them. A shadow consumer waits on the mask's `readyFence`, transitions its resources from COMMON for use, restores COMMON, and calls `RetainShadowMask` with its completion fence before releasing the shared frame. Submission and fence completion do not establish visual consumption or coverage.
